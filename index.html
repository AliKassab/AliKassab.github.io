About me

I currently work as a Game Developer at Mega Cat Studios. My journey started with Genesis Creations S.A.E.

My goal is to become a Creative Director: to shape the experiences we give players, build those visions alongside a team, and help the people I work with grow into their own creative strengths.

Programming is my craft. It's how I turn those ideas into something playable. Whether I'm building AI, systems, tools or gameplay architecture, technology is ultimately there to serve the experience.
